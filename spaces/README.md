# docchat — chat with your documents

Upload `.txt`/`.md` files, ask questions, get answers with cited sources.

- Free hosting on Hugging Face Spaces
- Answers generated by Groq's free tier (Llama) — no credit card required
- Your documents stay in your private account

## Usage

1. Sign up / log in (or use the demo account shown on the login screen).
2. Upload a `.txt` or `.md` file.
3. Ask a question — the answer streams in with sources.

> Note: on the free tier the app sleeps after ~48h idle and its storage is
> rebuilt on each redeploy, so this is a demo-grade deployment.
